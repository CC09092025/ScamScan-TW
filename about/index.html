About ScamScan

Recognizing scams starts with knowing what to look for.

ScamScan is an interactive educational platform designed to help people recognize scams, understand common deception techniques, and make more informed decisions online.

Why ScamScan exists

Scams are becoming increasingly convincing. Fraudulent emails, text messages, phone calls, and online listings can be difficult to distinguish from legitimate communications, especially when they imitate organizations and services people already trust.

Recognizing these threats requires more than simply knowing that scams exist. It requires understanding the tactics scammers use and knowing which warning signs to look for.

ScamScan aims to make learning those skills accessible through interactive examples and practical explanations.

An important distinction

ScamScan is an educational resource, not a scam detection service. Its scenarios and explanations are intended to help you develop better judgment, but they cannot guarantee that you will identify every fraudulent attempt. Always verify suspicious communications independently and contact organizations through their official websites or published contact information.
